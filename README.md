# STS-Public-Materials
A trading framework detailing market microstructure, backtesting, liquidity dynamics, and mechanical strategy design. Founded by the Sentient Trading Society.

## Provenance
STS Public Material - Our Public Education - https://SentientTradingSociety.com

Ron (SentientRon) and Ali (SentientAli) are the founders of the Sentient Trading Society. All STS public materials are written, reviewed, refined, and maintained directly by us.
The authors, Ron and Ali, have spent over 500 hours writing and refining these materials.
